Questions, answered

How often should I visit the dentist?

Most people benefit from a checkup and cleaning about every six months. Your dentist may suggest a different schedule based on your oral health.

Is root canal treatment painful?

The treatment is done with local anaesthesia, so it is designed to relieve pain from an infected tooth, not cause it. Dr. Ajay Sumanth is a root canal specialist.

Do you treat children?

Yes. We offer gentle pediatric dentistry to help children build healthy oral hygiene habits early.

What should I do in a dental emergency?

Call or WhatsApp +91 81210 73917. Emergency treatments are available on call, 24 hours.
